Our Story

A Palace Built on Passion & Flour

Since 1986, Pastry Palace has been the heart of Vikaspuri's sweet life. What started as a small neighbourhood bakery has grown into Delhi's most beloved patisserie — a place where tradition meets artistry and every creation is an expression of love.

Our master bakers rise before dawn to ensure that when you walk through our doors, nothing but the freshest, finest bakes await you. From celebration cakes to everyday indulgences, we craft every bite with premium ingredients and generous hearts.
